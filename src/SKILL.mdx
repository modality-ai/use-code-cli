---
name: "*use-code-cli"
description: "Supervise other Claude Code sessions from a Monitor tick: read state, detect progress, nudge, escalate, and stop."
---

> **Rule:** Always arm exactly one Monitor before any supervision work. No exceptions.
> **Rule:** Auto-assign each job to the hero whose skills best fit the work.
